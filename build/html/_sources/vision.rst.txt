Vision
======

It is clear that AI is going to transform the existing academic infrastructure
of mathematics. Introducing AI into a humanistic discipline such as
mathematics raises deep questions:

* What does it mean to do mathematics?
* What is the role of a mathematician in today's world?
* How will the culture of mathematical communities have to adapt?

Our aim
-------

We want to begin a discourse that helps students and early-career researchers
understand how the current political atmosphere surrounding AI directly
affects their academic paths and future job prospects. We also want to look
at the nuances of the technology itself and learn how to incorporate it
ethically into our work culture.

Topics
------

**AI safety**
   AI alignment and mitigation of misuse.

**Ethics of AI**
   Transparency and environmental impacts.

**Technicalities**
   Concepts used to train models to solve math-specific problems.

**Usage of AI**
   Practices for integrating AI into pedagogy.
