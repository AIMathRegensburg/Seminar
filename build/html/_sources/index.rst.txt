AI in Mathematics
=================
 
Over the past few months, the rapid wave of AI developments has disrupted the
usual way of doing mathematics. Reactions to this change vary widely, but the
future of students and early-career mathematicians is uncertain, obscured by
this new technological paradigm.
 
We are organising a series of events (a debate, a seminar, and possibly a
panel discussion) to start an open discussion about what this means for
students and early-career researchers: for their academic paths, their job
prospects, and the way mathematical communities work.
 
Read more about our :doc:`vision <vision>`, or :doc:`get in touch <contact>`.
